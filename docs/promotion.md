# Promotion plan

Set 2026-11-14. Zero budget, organic only, for Chinese readers in mainland China and overseas. Copy rules live in [content-brief.md](content-brief.md); this file holds the channels, the calendar, the keywords and the experiments.

## Decisions

1. Brand account on Xiaohongshu only, named 放假年龄. Zhihu answers come from the author's personal account. No WeChat official account.
2. Guide pages are hand-written, one a week, eight to ten in all. No pages generated per city or per amount.
3. Links that fill in a worked example use the address fragment (see content-brief.md), so the numbers never reach the server.
4. V2EX, Jike and GitHub posts may open with what was built; everywhere else the post leads with the experience.

## Why these channels

The domain has no ICP filing, so Baidu ranks it poorly. People in mainland China look for this kind of tool inside Xiaohongshu, Zhihu and Douyin rather than on Baidu. The plan therefore works on in-platform search, Google and Bing (Bing also has a share of desktop search in China and reaches readers overseas), and the 搜：放假年龄 line printed on every share card. The Xiaohongshu account exists so that this search finds something.

## Experiments

Traffic is too small for significance tests, so each experiment compares two periods or rotates content, with the bar set in advance. The funnel is `visit`, `calc_done`, `card_generate`, split by `ch`. The north-star number is weekly `calc_done`.

| ID | Hypothesis | Measure | Bar | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | A preview image raises link clicks | visits from `zh`, `v2`, `jk` | compared with later posts on the same channel | 4 weeks |
| E2 | One of the three Xiaohongshu hooks (Mondays, type, places) is clearly stronger | saves plus comments, direct visits | best at least twice the worst | W2–W7 |
| E3 | Fill-in links raise completion from Zhihu | `calc_done / visit` for `zh` (fill-in) and `zhp` (plain) | 30% higher | 4 weeks |
| E4 | The type question spreads by itself | share of comments naming a type | at least 40% | 2 posts |
| E5 | Guide pages bring search traffic | Search Console impressions | 500 a week from week 8 | 12 weeks |
| E6 | The share card drives visits | direct visits per `card_generate`, with 搜：放假年龄 against fire-age.com on the card | 20% more direct | 4 weeks, after E1 |
| E7 | Monthly data posts bring people back | visits that week against the week before | at least 10% | 3 months |
| E8 | Overseas readers complete more often | `calc_done / visit` for `1p3a`, `hr` | above the site average | 1 month |
